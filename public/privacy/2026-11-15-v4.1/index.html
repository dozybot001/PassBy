Privacy policy

Earlier version 4.1, effective on November 15, 2026 · Current version · Earlier version

Changes in this version: Android 2.1 adds AI grouping; this one feature connects to the network and is off by default.

PassBy is provided by Changsha Tenon Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. (长沙榫卯人工智能科技有限公司), the company behind Tenon AI ("we"). This policy covers the PassBy Android app from version 2.0; it will be updated to cover the iPhone app when version 2.0 for iPhone is released. The earlier web app and the iPhone version that synced through iCloud stopped being offered on November 15, 2026; see version 3.3. Contact us at support@tenonai.cn.

In short: no account, and your notebook never passes through our servers. Your phone keeps your notebook, and it moves with your phone's own backups and device transfers. The app stays offline by default; it connects to the network only when you turn on one feature, AI grouping. For what carries over on Android, see "Backups and new phones".

Your notebook

Entries (title, account, password, notes), folders, Recently Deleted, unfinished drafts and the date of your last export are kept in files in PassBy's private storage on this phone: the current file, and a copy left by the previous save, used to recover if the current file is damaged. Both are protected by the system's file encryption. PassBy adds no key of its own, so your notebook is as safe as your screen lock, fingerprint or face unlock; on a phone with no screen lock, file encryption gives no protection. Entries in Recently Deleted are kept on the phone for 30 days and removed the next time you open PassBy after that; you can delete them sooner. If a notebook file cannot be read, that file is renamed and left in private storage, outside backups. Uninstalling PassBy deletes all of these files.

Network

The Android app stays offline by default. It includes no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or update checks, and does not connect to our servers. From version 2.1 it requests network permission, used only for AI grouping, which is off until you turn it on; see the next section. Its only other permissions are for biometrics. Links to the privacy policy, terms and website open in your system browser only when you tap them; feedback is written in your email app, with the app version added to the subject and nothing else.

AI grouping

AI grouping is off by default. It uses the model provider you choose and your own API key: the service is provided by that provider, not by us, and PassBy only sends the request from your phone to it. The endpoint filled in by default is DeepSeek's; you can change it.

When you start a grouping, PassBy first shows you what will be sent. Only after you tap Send does it make one request from your phone straight to that provider. The request contains your entries' titles (up to 500 at a time; entries without a title are not sent), the names of your existing folders, a fixed piece of instruction text, and the app's name and version. The account, password and notes fields are never sent. The request does not pass through our servers, and we receive none of it; as with any online service, the provider sees your IP address.

What comes back is a set of suggestions: which folder each entry goes in, which titles are unclear and need your attention (placed in the “To clarify” folder), and tidier wording for titles. Nothing changes until you tick the suggestions you want and tap Apply, and you can undo it afterwards; no entry is deleted.

What you send is handled by the provider you chose under its own terms and privacy policy. We take no part in that and receive none of it; read its policy before you use it. If you choose a provider outside mainland China, this content is sent abroad. Anything you wrote into a title, such as an account name or a person's name, is sent along with the title.

Your API key is stored encrypted on this phone and is left out of system backups and export files. It is deleted when you turn AI grouping off, change the provider's address, or uninstall PassBy.

Unlocking, clipboard and screenshots

Your fingerprint, face and screen lock are verified by the system; PassBy receives only success or failure, never your biometric data. Tapping a row copies the password to the system clipboard and marks it as sensitive (on Android 13 and later it is not shown in the copy preview). It is cleared after about 60 seconds, possibly a little later while the phone is asleep; the app you paste into can read it. If your keyboard or your phone maker's clipboard history keeps its own copy, PassBy cannot clear that. PassBy blocks screenshots and screen recording, and hides its content in the recent apps view.

Backups and new phones

On Android 9 and later, your notebook is included in a cloud backup only when the system indicates that the backup can be end-to-end encrypted, which usually requires a screen lock; otherwise the system does not back up PassBy's data. End-to-end encryption is provided by the backup service, such as Google, as described in its documentation. A direct transfer between two phones brings your notebook along. On Android 8.0 and 8.1, PassBy is not included in system backups or in direct transfers between phones; use the encrypted export instead. We cannot verify whether phone makers' own cloud backup or transfer tools include PassBy or encrypt it; see the maker's documentation, or use PassBy's encrypted export. These backups are kept by the backup service or on your own devices and do not pass through us.

Encrypted export

An export file is created only when you choose Export. It contains all entries and folders, not Recently Deleted. The file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from your export code or your own passphrase using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. The file is saved where you choose, or handed to the app you choose. When you share it, a copy of the encrypted export file is kept temporarily in the app's cache; copies older than 15 minutes are deleted the next time you open PassBy or share again. We never receive export files and never store export codes or passphrases; without the code or passphrase the file cannot be opened.

Information we may receive

  • Support email: when you write to support@tenonai.cn, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and use them only to reply. See the Tenon AI Privacy Policy (https://tenonai.cn/privacy/) for how this is handled, where it is stored and how long it is kept. Please do not send passwords, export codes or device passcodes by email.
  • Website: when you visit passby.tenonai.cn, the server records access and security logs, including the time, IP address and page visited, never notebook contents. These logs are deleted automatically after 6 months. The server is in mainland China.
  • Downloads: the installer is hosted on PassBy's release pages on Gitee and GitHub. Downloads are handled by those platforms under their own privacy policies; we can see only download counts.

Data left by earlier versions

From version 2.0, PassBy no longer uses Tenon Account sign-in or iCloud sync.

  • The session records and identity details kept by the earlier sign-in service were all deleted on November 15, 2026. Your Tenon Account itself is not affected.
  • The encrypted notebook that earlier versions synced to your iCloud stays in your own iCloud; we cannot read or delete it. You can delete PassBy's data under Settings › your name › iCloud › Storage on your iPhone.
  • The authorization key and preferences the earlier web app saved in your browser can be removed by clearing the site data for passby.tenonai.cn and passby.tenon.asia in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can access, copy, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or ask us to explain how we handle it. You manage your notebook yourself in the app; for anything else, email support@tenonai.cn and we will reply within 15 working days.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the version and effective date here and keep earlier versions available.

隐私政策

历史版本（第 4.1 版），2026 年 11 月 15 日有效 · 当前版本 · 更早版本

本版改动：安卓 2.1 增加 AI 分组，这一个功能会联网，默认关闭。

PassBy 由长沙榫卯人工智能科技有限公司（品牌「榫卯 Tenon AI」，下称「我们」）提供。本政策适用于 PassBy 2.0 及以后的安卓应用；iPhone 版 2.0 发布时，本政策会更新并加上它。旧网页版和通过 iCloud 同步的旧版 iPhone 应用已于 2026 年 11 月 15 日停止提供，见第 3.3 版。联系方式：support@tenonai.cn。

一句话：没有账号，不经过我们的服务器；密码本由手机保管，跟着手机自己的备份和换机走。App 默认不联网，只有你打开「AI 分组」这一个功能时才联网。安卓上哪些情况带得走，见「系统备份和换手机」。

你的密码本

条目（标题、账号、密码、备注）、分组、「最近删除」、没保存完的草稿和上次导出日期，存在这台手机上 PassBy 私有目录的文件里：当前的一份，和上一次保存时留下的一份副本（当前文件损坏时用来恢复），都由系统的文件加密保护。PassBy 不另加自己的密钥，所以密码本的安全取决于你的锁屏密码、指纹或人脸；手机没有设锁屏时，文件加密起不到保护作用。「最近删除」里的条目在本机保留 30 天，满 30 天后在你下一次打开 PassBy 时移除，你也可以提前删除。密码本文件读不出时，读不出的那份会改名留在私有目录里，不进备份。卸载 PassBy 会删掉本机的这些文件。

联网

安卓应用默认不联网，不带广告、统计、崩溃上报或更新检查，不连我们的服务器。从 2.1 起，应用申请联网权限，只用于「AI 分组」；这个功能默认关闭，由你手动打开，见下一节。除此之外它只申请生物识别权限。隐私政策、用户协议和官网的链接，只在你点了以后用系统浏览器打开；反馈用你的邮件应用写，标题自动带上应用版本，不带别的信息。

AI 分组

AI 分组默认关闭。它用的是你自己选的模型服务商和你自己的 API Key：这项服务由服务商提供，不是我们提供的，PassBy 只负责把请求从你的手机发给它。默认填的是 DeepSeek 的接口地址，你可以换成别的。

你开始一次分组时，PassBy 先给你看将要发送的内容，你点「发送」后，才从你的手机直接向这家服务商发出一次请求。请求里有：条目的标题（一次最多 500 个，没有标题的条目不发送）、你现有文件夹的名称、一段固定的说明文字，以及应用的名称和版本号。账户、密码和备注这三栏不会发送。请求不经过我们的服务器，我们收不到其中的任何内容；和访问任何网络服务一样，服务商会看到你的 IP 地址。

模型返回的是建议：条目分到哪个文件夹，哪些标题不清楚、需要你补充（放进「待明确」文件夹），以及标题的规范写法。你勾选要采用的建议并点「应用」后才改动，应用后可以撤销；不会删除条目。

发出去的内容由你选的服务商按它自己的协议和隐私政策处理，我们不参与，也收不到；使用前请先看它的政策。你选境外的服务商时，这些内容会发往境外。标题里如果写了账号、姓名之类的信息，会随标题一起发出。

API Key 加密保存在这台手机上，不进系统备份和导出文件；你关闭 AI 分组、换了服务商的地址或卸载 PassBy 时删除。

解锁、剪贴板和截屏

指纹、人脸和锁屏密码由系统验证，PassBy 只收到成功或失败，收不到你的生物特征。点一行会把密码复制到系统剪贴板，并标为敏感内容（Android 13 及以上不在复制预览里显示），约 60 秒后清除，手机休眠时可能稍晚；你粘贴进去的应用能读到它。输入法或手机厂商的剪贴板历史如果另外记了一份，PassBy 清除不了。PassBy 禁止截屏和录屏，多任务界面不显示内容。

系统备份和换手机

在 Android 9 及以上，只有系统表示这次云端备份能端到端加密时（一般要求手机设了锁屏），密码本才进入云端备份；不能加密时，系统不备份 PassBy 的数据。端到端加密由提供备份的服务（例如 Google）完成，以它的说明为准。两台手机直接传输时，密码本照常带走。Android 8.0 和 8.1 上，PassBy 不进系统备份，两台手机直接传输也带不走，请用加密导出。国内手机厂商自己的云备份和换机工具，我们无法验证会不会包含 PassBy、是否加密，请以厂商的说明为准，或者用 PassBy 的加密导出。这些备份由提供备份的服务或你的设备保存，不经过我们。

加密导出

只有你选择「导出」时才生成导出文件，内容是全部条目和分组，不含「最近删除」。文件用 AES-256-GCM 加密，密钥由导出码或你设的口令经 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 派生。文件存到你选的位置，或交给你选的应用。分享时，加密后的导出文件会在应用缓存里临时留一份；下一次打开 PassBy 或再次分享时，留了超过 15 分钟的会被删除。我们收不到导出文件，也不保存导出码和口令，丢了就打不开这个文件。

我们可能收到的信息

  • 支持邮件：你发邮件到 support@tenonai.cn 时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和你附上的信息，只用来答复你。处理方式、存放地点和保存期限见 Tenon AI 隐私政策（https://tenonai.cn/zh/privacy/）。请不要在邮件里发送密码、导出码或设备密码。
  • 网站：访问 passby.tenonai.cn 时，服务器会记录访问日志和安全日志，包括访问时间、IP 地址和访问的页面，不含密码本内容；这些日志保存 6 个月后自动删除。服务器位于中国境内。
  • 下载：安装包放在 Gitee 和 GitHub 上 PassBy 的发布页，下载由这两个平台处理，适用它们各自的隐私政策。我们只能看到下载次数。

旧版本留下的数据

从 2.0 起，PassBy 不再使用 Tenon 账号登录和 iCloud 同步。

  • 旧版登录服务保存过的会话记录和身份信息，已于 2026 年 11 月 15 日全部删除。Tenon 账号本身不受影响。
  • 旧版同步到你 iCloud 里的加密密码本，留在你自己的 iCloud 里，我们无法读取或删除。你可以在 iPhone 的「设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 储存空间」里删除 PassBy 的数据。
  • 旧网页版在你浏览器里保存的授权密钥和偏好，可以在浏览器设置里清除 passby.tenonai.cn 和 passby.tenon.asia 的网站数据。

你的权利

你可以查阅、复制、更正、删除你的个人信息，撤回同意，或要求我们解释处理规则。密码本在 App 里由你自己管理；其他请求请发邮件至 support@tenonai.cn，我们会在 15 个工作日内答复。

本政策的变更

本政策修改时，我们会在本页更新版本和生效日期，并保留历史版本。
