Privacy policy

Version 3.3 · Effective November 15, 2026 · Previous version

Changes in this version: the web app and the iPhone version that synced through iCloud are no longer offered; this version explains what happens to data afterwards.

PassBy is provided by Changsha Tenon Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. (长沙榫卯人工智能科技有限公司), the company behind Tenon AI ("we"). Contact us at support@tenonai.cn.

Where things stand

The PassBy web app was retired on November 15, 2026. The iPhone version that synced through iCloud (1.3 and earlier) has been removed from the App Store and will not be updated. PassBy's sign-in service was shut down on November 15, 2026. A new PassBy is being prepared, with no account and a notebook kept on your phone; this policy will be updated when it is released. For how earlier versions handled information, see the previous version.

An earlier iPhone app you already installed

A notebook already on your iPhone still opens with Face ID or Touch ID. It is still encrypted on your device and stored in your private iCloud database, as the previous version describes. Now that the sign-in service is shut down you can no longer sign in.

Your notebook

Your notebook has always been in your own private iCloud database, under your Apple Account. We cannot read it, and it is not deleted by this change. To delete it, delete the entries in the earlier app, or delete PassBy's data under Settings › your name › iCloud › Storage on your iPhone.

Data on our servers

PassBy's sign-in service kept session token hashes, verified identity details (Tenon Account identifier, display name, email, platform role) and session expiry times. It never held notebooks or recovery keys. The sign-in service is shut down, and all of these records were deleted on November 15, 2026. Server access logs (time, IP address, request path) are kept for six months as the law requires and then deleted. Your Tenon Account is not affected; manage or delete it at Tenon Account.

What was left in your browser

In a browser you authorized, the earlier web app left a non-exportable decryption key and your sign-in preferences. Clear the site data for passby.tenonai.cn and the old address passby.tenon.asia in your browser settings to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or ask about this policy, at support@tenonai.cn. We reply within fifteen working days.

简体中文

隐私政策

第 3.3 版 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日 · 上一版本

本版改动：网页版和通过 iCloud 同步的 iPhone 版停止提供，说明停止后各项数据怎么处理。

PassBy 由长沙榫卯人工智能科技有限公司（品牌「榫卯 Tenon AI」，下称「我们」）提供。联系方式：support@tenonai.cn。

现在的情况

PassBy 网页版已于 2026 年 11 月 15 日停用。通过 iCloud 同步的 iPhone 版（1.3 及更早）已从 App Store 下架，不再更新。PassBy 的登录服务已于 2026 年 11 月 15 日停用。新版 PassBy 正在准备，没有账号，密码本存在你的手机上；它发布时本政策会更新。旧版本怎样处理信息，见上一版本。

已经装好的旧版 iPhone 应用

已有的密码本仍可以用 Face ID 或触控 ID 打开，仍在你的设备上加密、存放在你的 iCloud 私有数据库，这部分和上一版本所述相同。登录服务已停用，不能再登录。

你的密码本

密码本一直在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库里，由你的 Apple 账号控制，我们读不到，也不会因为这次停用被删除。想删除的，在旧版应用里删除条目，或在 iPhone 的「设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 储存空间」里删除 PassBy 的数据。

我们服务器上的数据

PassBy 的登录服务保存过会话令牌的哈希、经验证的身份信息（Tenon 账号标识、显示名、邮箱、平台角色）和会话到期时间，从不保存密码本和恢复密钥。登录服务已停用，这些记录已于 2026 年 11 月 15 日全部删除。服务器的访问日志（时间、IP 地址、请求路径）依法保留六个月后删除。你的 Tenon 账号不受影响，可在 Tenon 账号页管理或删除。

浏览器里留下的

在授权过的浏览器里，旧网页版留有一把不可导出的解密密钥和登录偏好。在浏览器设置里清除 passby.tenonai.cn 和旧地址 passby.tenon.asia 的网站数据即可删除。

你的权利

你可以查询、更正、删除我们持有的你的个人信息，或对本政策提出疑问：写信到 support@tenonai.cn，我们在十五个工作日内答复。
