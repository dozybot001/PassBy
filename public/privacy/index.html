Privacy policy

Version 3 · Effective November 7, 2026 · Previous version

PassBy is provided by Changsha Tenon Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. (长沙榫卯人工智能科技有限公司), the company behind Tenon AI ("we"). We handle the personal information described in this policy. Contact us at support@tenonai.cn.

Your notebook

Titles, accounts, passwords, notes and folders are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before they are synced to your private iCloud database. PassBy has no developer-operated password server, advertising or analytics SDK.

Tenon Account

Tenon manages sign-in methods, verification and account settings. PassBy receives your verified Tenon identifier, display name and platform role. Web and updated iPhone account sessions last up to 30 days after verification. Older iPhone versions must be updated and signed in again to use the current account service. Web sessions do not renew silently. The iPhone renews its session using a rotating credential kept in the device-only Keychain, never synced to iCloud. Each sign-in and renewal verifies your current central identity, role and verified email. The credential expires after 30 days without renewal; signing out removes it from this iPhone and requests central revocation. Closing the app does not sign you out. Offline, the remembered account profile does not grant renewed account privileges; your existing notebook still opens with Face ID. The authentication service stores only product session token hashes, verified identity details, fixed expiry and optional refresh credential hashes in private SQLite. Normal restarts preserve the original deadline; expired or signed-out sessions are removed. Central tokens, native refresh credential plaintext and one-use login tickets are processed transiently without logging or persisting them to disk. It never receives notebook passwords or recovery keys. Platform roles do not grant access to notebook contents. Routine security and request metadata may be retained by the hosting and central account services. Manage your central account at Tenon Account.

Account information

Tenon processes your email, display name, account identifier and login security records for authentication and account management. PassBy receives verified identity details to establish its product session. These details are linked to your account and are not used for advertising or tracking. Notebook contents and recovery keys are never sent to Tenon. See the Tenon Account privacy notice for central account retention and your rights.

Apple storage and Face ID

Your system iCloud account identifies the notebook. PassBy does not receive your Apple password or facial data. Apple may share identity information with Tenon when you choose Apple as a central sign-in method; this is separate from notebook storage authorization. Face ID is handled by iOS; PassBy receives authentication results, never your facial data.

Keys and recovery

The local encryption key is protected by the device Keychain and current Face ID or Touch ID enrollment. Your recovery key is never uploaded. Save it outside PassBy. Without a working device key or your recovery key, your notebook cannot be decrypted by the developer.

Clipboard and drafts

In the iPhone app, copied values stay on this device and expire after 60 seconds. The app you paste into can read them. Unfinished entries are saved as encrypted local drafts and are not synced to iCloud.

Web access

The web app connects directly to Apple CloudKit for your private notebook. Your recovery key is used by WebCrypto in the browser and is not sent to our hosting server or Apple. Notebook data is decrypted in page memory; the recovery phrase, notebook contents and unfinished edits are not saved to browser storage. Authorizing a browser saves a non-exportable decryption key locally, scoped to your Tenon identity and Apple storage account. Remove it with “Forget this browser” in Settings. This browser storage is not protected by iPhone Face ID. The browser stores only your optional “keep Apple signed in” preference. If you enable it on a trusted computer, Apple’s SDK also persists an authentication token; signing out through “Change Apple Account” clears that token. An authorized browser can open the notebook during a valid Tenon session; a new browser still needs initial authorization with the recovery key. Switching tabs, leaving the window or 5 minutes of inactivity locks the notebook and removes its contents from the interface. Browsers cannot provide the iPhone app’s Face ID or clipboard expiry. Use a trusted computer. Our web host receives routine page requests and service metadata such as IP addresses; it does not receive notebook contents or recovery keys.

Retention and deletion

Choose Delete account in Settings, or open account deletion. Tenon verifies your identity, then you choose the scope. Deleting only PassBy removes your PassBy sessions and its link to your Tenon identity, and keeps your Tenon Account for other products. Deleting your Tenon Account removes central account information and revokes available Apple sign-in tokens, which affects all Tenon products. Neither deletes your Apple-stored notebook or recovery key, which are managed separately. PassBy keeps a record that the deletion happened, holding only a hashed account identifier, so a repeated request cannot delete an account you create later.

Delete entries and folders inside PassBy and allow iCloud sync to finish to apply those deletions to your notebook. Each iPhone keeps deleted passwords encrypted in Recently Deleted for 30 days without syncing them; you can delete them sooner there. Removing the app deletes its local files, but does not delete the iCloud notebook. Apple manages iCloud storage and service metadata under its own privacy policy.

PassBy sign-in sessions last up to 30 days. Signed-out and rotated sign-in credentials are remembered only as hashes for 30 days, so they cannot be reused. To keep our network secure, our servers record access and security logs: the time, IP address and page requested, without URL parameters. These logs are deleted automatically after 6 months.

Service providers and location

PassBy shares data only with the services it needs: Tenon Account for sign-in, operated by the same publisher; Apple for iCloud storage under your Apple Account and, if you choose it, Sign in with Apple; and our hosting provider. The PassBy server is in mainland China, and PassBy does not send your personal information outside mainland China. Apple stores your notebook according to your Apple Account region, and the Tenon Account privacy notice covers Apple sign-in.

Support and your choices

View and change your notebook in the app, and your account details in Tenon Account. Delete your account as described above. You can contact support@tenonai.cn for technical or privacy questions, or to ask what we hold about you. Any details you voluntarily send are used to answer your request. Never send passwords, recovery keys, Apple verification codes or device passcodes.

你的密码本

标题、账户、密码、备注及文件夹在设备上使用 AES-256-GCM 加密后，再同步至你的 iCloud 私有数据库。PassBy 不使用开发者运营的密码服务器、广告或统计 SDK。

Tenon 账号

Tenon 统一处理登录方式、验证与账号设置。PassBy 接收已验证的 Tenon 用户标识、称呼及平台角色。网页与新版 iPhone 账号会话均在验证成功后有效 30 天。旧 iPhone 版本需要更新并重新登录，才能使用当前账号服务。网页不静默续期。iPhone 使用保存在本机钥匙串中的轮换凭据续期，不同步至 iCloud；每次登录和续期重新验证当前中央身份、角色与已验证邮箱。凭据在 30 天未续期后到期；主动退出会从本机移除，并请求中央服务撤销。关闭应用不会退出账号。离线保存的账号资料不授予新的账号权限，已有密码本仍可通过 Face ID 打开。认证服务仅将产品会话令牌摘要、已验证身份、固定到期时间及可选续期摘要保存在私有 SQLite 中。正常重启保留原到期时间，过期或退出后移除会话。中央令牌、手机续期凭据原文及一次性登录票据只在内存中临时处理，不记录或保存至磁盘；该服务不接收密码本密码或恢复密钥。平台角色不授予密码本内容访问权。网页主机及中央账号服务可能保存常规安全与请求信息。中央账号可在 Tenon 账号中心管理。

账号信息

Tenon 为身份验证与账号管理处理邮箱、称呼、账号标识及登录安全记录。PassBy 接收已验证的身份信息，建立产品会话。这些信息关联账号，不用于广告或追踪。密码本内容与恢复密钥不会发送给 Tenon。中央账号的保存期限与权利说明见 Tenon 账号隐私说明。

Apple 存储与 Face ID

密码本通过系统 iCloud 账号识别。PassBy 不获取你的 Apple 密码。若选择 Apple 作为中央登录方式，Apple 可向 Tenon 提供身份信息；这与密码本存储授权分别处理。Face ID 由 iOS 处理，应用只接收验证结果，不接收面部数据。

密钥与恢复

本机加密密钥受设备钥匙串和当前 Face ID 或 Touch ID 登记保护。恢复密钥不会上传，请在 PassBy 之外保存。若设备密钥失效且恢复密钥丢失，开发者无法解密密码本。

剪贴板与草稿

iPhone 应用中，复制的内容仅保留在本机，60 秒后过期。接收粘贴的应用可以读取内容。未完成的条目以加密草稿保存在本机，不同步至 iCloud。

网页版访问

网页版直接连接 Apple CloudKit 中你的私有密码本。恢复密钥由浏览器的 WebCrypto 使用，不会发送至我们的网页服务器或 Apple。密码本只在页面内存中解密；恢复密钥原文、条目及未保存的编辑不会写入浏览器存储。授权浏览器后，本地保存不可导出的解密密钥，按 Tenon 身份与 Apple 存储账号隔离；可在设置中选择“撤销这台浏览器的授权”。浏览器密钥存储不受 iPhone Face ID 保护。浏览器仅保存“保持 Apple 登录”的可选偏好。若在可信电脑启用该选项，Apple SDK 还会保存身份验证令牌；通过“更换 Apple 账号”退出会清除此令牌。已授权的浏览器可在 Tenon 登录有效时直接打开；新浏览器仍需首次用恢复密钥授权。切换标签页、离开窗口或 5 分钟无操作时自动锁定，并从界面移除内容。浏览器无法提供 iPhone 应用的 Face ID 或剪贴板过期能力，请使用可信的电脑。网页服务器会接收常规页面请求及 IP 地址等服务信息，不接收密码本内容或恢复密钥。

保存与删除

可在设置中选择“删除账号”，或打开账号删除页面。Tenon 重新验证身份后，由你选择删除范围。仅删除 PassBy：移除 PassBy 的登录会话及其与 Tenon 身份的关联，保留 Tenon 账号供其他产品使用。删除 Tenon 账号：删除中央账号资料并撤销可用的 Apple 登录令牌，会影响所有 Tenon 产品。两种方式都不会删除 Apple 私有存储中的密码本与恢复密钥，它们分别管理。PassBy 会保留一条已删除的记录，只含账号标识的摘要，使重复的请求不会删除你之后新建的账号。

在 PassBy 中删除条目和文件夹后，请等待 iCloud 同步完成，使删除同步至密码本。每台 iPhone 会在「最近删除」中加密保留已删除的密码 30 天，不会同步；你也可以在那里提前删除。卸载应用会删除本机文件，不会删除 iCloud 密码本。Apple 按其隐私政策管理 iCloud 存储及服务元数据。

PassBy 登录会话最长 30 天。已退出或已轮换的登录凭据只以摘要形式记录 30 天，防止被再次使用。为保障网络安全，服务器会记录访问日志和安全日志，包括访问时间、IP 地址和访问的页面，不含网址中的参数；这些日志保存 6 个月后自动删除。

服务提供方与所在地

PassBy 只与运行所需的服务共享数据：Tenon 账号负责登录，与 PassBy 同一出品方；Apple 按你的 Apple 账号提供 iCloud 存储，并在你选择时提供 Apple 登录；以及我们的服务器托管商。PassBy 服务器位于中国境内，PassBy 不向境外提供你的个人信息。密码本由 Apple 按你的 Apple 账号所在地区存储；Apple 登录的处理见 Tenon 账号隐私说明。

支持与你的选择

密码本可在应用内查看和修改，账号资料可在 Tenon 账号中心查看和修改，删除方式见上文。如有技术或隐私问题，或想了解我们保存了你的哪些信息，可联系 support@tenonai.cn。你主动提供的信息仅用于处理支持请求。请勿发送密码、恢复密钥、Apple 验证码或设备密码。
