PassBy

A simple password notebook. No account, and nothing goes through our servers: your notebook is kept on your phone.

On your computer

Use your notebook in Chrome or Edge on a computer that is on the same Wi-Fi as your phone. In PassBy on the phone, open Settings › Sync and tap “Let another device connect”, then enter the address and pairing code it shows. You can view, search, copy and edit, and every change is saved back to the phone. What is sent is encrypted and goes only between the two devices; the notebook is not stored in the browser.

Open PassBy on the web

On your phone

PassBy for Android is available on GitHub and Gitee; check the installer as described on the Support page. The iPhone version is being prepared.

Earlier version

The earlier web app, which needed a Tenon account, and the iPhone version that synced through iCloud were retired on 15 November 2026. Your notebook in iCloud is not affected; it stays in your own iCloud. If you used the earlier version and need help with your notebook, write to support@tenonai.cn.

PassBy

一个简单的密码本。没有账号，不经过我们的服务器，密码本存在你的手机上。

在电脑上用

电脑和手机连同一个 Wi-Fi，用 Chrome 或 Edge 打开网页版。在手机的 PassBy 里打开「设置 › 同步」，点「让别的设备连过来」，把显示的地址和配对码输到网页里。可以查看、搜索、复制和修改，每次改动都当场存回手机。传输的内容加密后只在这两台设备之间传，浏览器里不保存密码本。

打开网页版

在手机上用

安卓版已发布，安装包在 GitHub 和 Gitee，安装前请按支持页的办法核对；iPhone 版正在准备。

旧版

需要 Tenon 账号的旧网页版和通过 iCloud 同步的 iPhone 版已于 2026 年 11 月 15 日停用。你在 iCloud 里的密码本不受影响，仍在你自己的 iCloud 里。用过旧版、需要取回密码本的，请写信到 support@tenonai.cn。
